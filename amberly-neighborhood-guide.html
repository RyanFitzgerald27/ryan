The first time I showed a home in Amberly, my buyers were relocating from New York for a position at RTP. They pulled in and said, "This feels like a resort." I have heard some version of that reaction from almost every buyer I have brought here since. After selling homes in this neighborhood for over ten years, I can tell you exactly why it keeps that reputation.

How Amberly Was Built and Why It Still Works

Amberly was planned in 1995 when west Cary was farmland. The idea was a mixed use community where people could work, shop, go to school, and walk greenways around a central park and 16 acre lake. Today it covers over 1,100 acres with more than 5,100 homes planned across eight sub neighborhoods, plus a 75 acre Town Center for commercial and office space.

What separates Amberly from places like Preston, Carpenter Village, or Scotts Mill is the range. Townhomes start in the low $400,000s. A four bedroom in Amberly Glen runs $600,000 to $800,000 or more. Carolina Preserve, the Del Webb 55+ section, is built right into the same community. That kind of variety under one HOA is not common in Cary.

What Buyers Ask Me About Amberly

The most common question is about the HOA dues. My advice: walk the clubhouse before you decide. The club includes a fitness center, Jr. Olympic pool, family pool, aerobics room, children's playroom, teen room, and event lawn. Price that out separately and the HOA is a good deal.

Schools drive most buying decisions here. Most homes are zoned for Mills Park Elementary, Mills Park Middle, and Panther Creek High School. Panther Creek carries an A+ rating from Niche and is ranked among the top high schools in the state. A small slice of Amberly falls into the Chatham County school district. I check exact school assignments for every buyer before we write an offer because the boundary line runs through the neighborhood. That detail gets missed by agents who do not work this area regularly.

Location is straightforward. Amberly sits off NC 540 and Highway 55. That puts you 15 minutes from RTP, 20 minutes from RDU Airport, and 10 minutes from Parkside Town Commons and Beaver Creek Commons for shopping and restaurants. Buyers who commute to both Raleigh and Durham tell me this is the spot that makes the drive work in both directions.

What Sellers Should Know

I have listed dozens of homes in Amberly. The lots that back up to the greenway or the lake sell for $30,000 to $50,000 more than the same floor plan on an interior lot. Buyers in this price range expect the home to show well, so staging and professional photography matter. The listings that sell fastest are the ones priced correctly from day one with a strong first week push.

Timing matters in Amberly. Spring is the strongest season because families want to close before school starts. But fall is underrated here. RTP relocations do not follow the school calendar, and I have seen solid activity in September and October when inventory is lower and serious buyers are still looking.

What It Is Like to Live Here

The Amberly Wellness Park and Morris Branch Greenway give you about two miles of connected trails. I have run them. They are maintained well and quiet enough to feel like you are not in a subdivision. The community runs events through the year, from pool nights to seasonal get togethers that actually draw people out.

Old Chatham Golf Club borders the community with 400 acres of golf if that is your thing. Parkside Town Commons is minutes away for groceries, restaurants, and everyday errands. People who live here describe it as quiet, safe, and well kept. That matches what I have seen over the years.

Common Questions

Amberly FAQ

What are homes in Amberly worth?

Townhomes start in the low $400,000s. Single family homes in Amberly Glen sell between $500,000 and $800,000 or more depending on size, lot, and upgrades. Greenway and lake lots sell at a premium. Check the active listings above and recent sold data for the most current numbers.

What schools serve Amberly?

Most homes are zoned for Mills Park Elementary, Mills Park Middle or Alston Ridge Middle, and Panther Creek High School or Green Hope High School through Wake County Public Schools. A small section falls into Chatham County schools. I verify exact assignments for my clients because the boundary runs through the neighborhood and can shift.

Does Amberly have an HOA and what does it cover?

Yes. The HOA manages the pools, clubhouse, fitness center, playgrounds, greenway trails, and community events. Dues vary by sub neighborhood. For what you get, it is one of the better HOA values in Cary.

Is Amberly a good place to buy?

Amberly has held value and appreciated steadily. That is supported by strong school zones, consistent RTP relocation demand, and limited comparable inventory in west Cary. No investment is guaranteed, but the fundamentals are as strong as any neighborhood I sell in across the Triangle.
